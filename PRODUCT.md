# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Small used-device shop owners and operators who manage iPad intake, pricing, inventory, orders, repair costs, customer follow-up, and daily profit from a mobile or tablet device. They need to scan the business in seconds, then jump into the next operational action.

## Product Purpose

This app is an operations cockpit for used iPad resale. It should make daily GMV, gross profit, inventory exposure, stale stock, pending orders, and core actions visible without visual noise. Success means the user can answer "what needs attention now?" and "what should I do next?" from the first screen.

## Brand Personality

Precise, calm, practical.

## Anti-references

Avoid marketing-style hero layouts, oversized cards, emoji-driven navigation, decorative gradients, nested cards, and dashboards that look impressive but slow down scanning. Avoid playful consumer-app styling when the screen is used for repeated business work.

## Design Principles

1. Put operational facts before decoration.
2. Keep the first viewport dense but readable.
3. Use one consistent component vocabulary across all pages.
4. Make primary actions easy to reach, but visually quieter than the data.
5. Favor predictable mobile/tablet patterns over novelty.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target readable contrast for normal text, 44px or larger touch targets, clear pressed and selected states, and layouts that tolerate Chinese labels without clipping. Motion should communicate state only and must not block use.
